Questions

Before you sign up

Is CampusLink free for students?

Yes. Creating an account, browsing and booking cost nothing. You only pay for the services and items you order.

How do payments work?

You top up your CampusLink wallet with M-Pesa and pay from there. You can also send money to another student or withdraw back to M-Pesa.

Who can offer services or post gigs?

Any registered student, campus business or society. Choose “Provider” or “Recruiter” when you create your account.

What happens if a service goes wrong?

Every booking can be rated and reviewed. Providers with poor reviews are flagged and can be removed by campus admins.
